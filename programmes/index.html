Most golfers do not struggle because they are unwilling to practise. The harder problem is knowing what to do, how long to do it for and whether the work is actually moving in the right direction. A saved video, a list of drills or an occasional burst of enthusiasm can all be useful, but they rarely create a repeatable process. ForeThought Guided Programmes are designed to close that gap. Each programme has a defined purpose, a sensible sequence and individual sessions that can be completed and recorded inside the ForeThought ecosystem.

The programme is supplied as a small JSON file. JSON is simply the format the app uses to understand the programme; you do not need to open it, edit it or know anything technical. After downloading the file, import it into the supported ForeThought or Lesson Logger app. The app turns it into a guided experience containing the programme introduction, session order, exercises or drills, coaching notes, targets and the information needed to record completed work.

This approach keeps the programme portable. It is not trapped inside an online account, and it does not disappear when a subscription ends. The file belongs to you and can be stored alongside your other golf data. Once loaded, the programme can be used when you are at the range, on a practice green, at home or in the gym. Where the app supports offline use, the programme remains available without a data connection.

Practice programmes concentrate on golf skills: putting, wedge control, iron play, strike, dispersion and the decisions that help those skills transfer to the course. Fitness programmes concentrate on the physical work that supports golf: useful mobility, strength, control, resilience and conditioning. The two areas are separate because they ask different things of the player, but they share the same principle—every session should have a reason, a structure and a way of showing what was completed.

Each product page explains who the programme is for, what it aims to improve, the equipment required and the expected time commitment. You can review the structure before buying, then download the programme file when checkout is complete. Free demonstration programmes can be downloaded directly. Paid downloads will be connected to Stripe, with a simple purchase flow and no need to create a second account just to obtain the file.

Guided Programmes are not intended to replace a coach, physiotherapist or qualified trainer. They provide structure between lessons and make independent practice more deliberate. Start at a level that suits you, follow the guidance supplied with the programme and stop if an exercise causes pain. The goal is not to collect programmes. It is to choose the right piece of work, complete it consistently and give your golf a better chance of improving.

01 / Skill development

Practice

Practice programmes give each range bucket, short-game session or hour on the putting green a clear job.

Every programme begins with an outcome rather than a collection of fashionable drills. Sessions are ordered so that you can establish a baseline, work on the underlying skill and gradually introduce variation, decision-making and pressure. That might mean building a dependable wedge matrix before testing random distances, improving start-line control before playing scored putting games, or understanding your iron dispersion before changing how you select targets.

The programme lives inside the app, where individual drills include setup guidance, instructions, targets and space to record the result. This creates a useful history without turning practice into administration. You can see what has been completed, return to earlier sessions and repeat an assessment when you want to measure change.

These programmes are made for ordinary practice facilities and realistic schedules. Equipment requirements are kept clear, session times are shown in advance and alternatives are included where the programme permits them. Use them alongside coaching, as structured homework after a lesson or as a way to bring purpose back to independent practice.

02 / Physical preparation

Fitness

Fitness programmes organise the work that helps you move, practise and play with greater confidence.

Golf asks for rotation, balance, force and control across a wide range of positions. It also asks the body to repeat those movements through practice sessions and long rounds. The guided fitness catalogue turns those broad demands into manageable programmes with a defined weekly rhythm. Rather than guessing which exercises belong together, you can open the next session and see the intended movement, sets, repetitions, coaching notes and available substitutions.

Some programmes focus on accessible mobility and movement quality. Others introduce progressive strength or conditioning for golfers who already train regularly. Product pages clearly state the intended level, equipment and approximate duration so that you can choose something appropriate instead of buying a plan based on its title alone. Completed work is recorded in the fitness area, keeping it alongside the wider picture of your golf activity and recovery.

The programmes are general training resources, not medical treatment or individual clinical advice. If you have an injury, health condition or uncertainty about an exercise, speak to an appropriately qualified professional before starting. Progress comes from suitable work performed consistently—not from forcing a movement or chasing somebody else’s numbers.
